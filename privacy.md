# Privacy Policy for Block Nova

*Last updated: October 20, 2026*

Block Nova ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game made by **Adithya Anne** ("we", "us").

## Summary
- Your game progress stays on your device. We don't run servers and we don't collect your name, email or account details.
- The app shows **optional rewarded video ads** from **Google AdMob**. You only see an ad when you choose to tap "Watch ad". Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

## What the app stores on your device
The app saves the following **only on your own device**, in the app's private storage:
- your best scores, level, progress, achievements and game statistics,
- your settings (music, sound, vibration, screen shake),
- your current unfinished games, so you can continue them later.

We cannot see this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded video ads. When the app requests or shows an ad, **Google** may automatically collect and process:
- your device's **advertising ID** and app set ID,
- your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location,
- **interactions with ads** (such as ad views and taps) and app launch information,
- **diagnostic information** (such as performance and crash data).

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. Google may show personalised ads where the law allows and you have agreed.

- **European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland:** the first time you open the app, a consent message from Google lets you choose how your data may be used for ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's **Settings → Privacy Options**.
- **Everyone:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state** — needed only to load ads. The game itself works offline.
- **Vibration** — short haptic feedback during play; you can turn it off in Settings.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
